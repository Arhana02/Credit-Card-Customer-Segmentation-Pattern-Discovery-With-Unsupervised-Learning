# Credit-Card-Customer-Segmentation-Pattern-Discovery-With-Unsupervised-Learning
📌 Project Overview
Customer behavior in the banking industry varies significantly across financial activity, credit card usage, digital engagement, branch visits, and customer service interactions. Treating all customers with the same marketing and engagement strategy may therefore result in missed opportunities for personalization.
This project focuses on **customer segmentation** for **AllLife Bank** using unsupervised learning techniques. The objective is to identify meaningful groups of customers based on their financial and banking behavior and translate these segments into actionable business strategies.
Two clustering techniques were applied and compared:
- K Means Clustering
- Hierarchical Clustering
The analysis ultimately identified distinct customer groups that can support targeted marketing, personalized banking services, customer retention, and improved operational efficiency.
